Dice Grid · dice on the table

Throw the dice into the tray. The tray is the same size as the grid and split into the same cells, and wherever the white die comes to rest picks the one cell that changes. Instead of pips it shows bands: its face is the tile that cell will get, with the same number of bands starting from the same corner, and it turns as the die turns. How far it sits off square sets the tilt: a die lying a little crooked gives a slightly tilted tile (up to ±7.5°, while Tilt is on). The grey die shows a colour sample instead of pips: whichever way up it lands, light ground with a dark band or dark ground with a light band, is how that cell is coloured. Where it lands doesn't matter. Throw with the button or Space, or press in the tray and flick.

Drag the mark on the grid, or use the sliders. Distances are from the grid's centre in real millimetres (+X right, +Y down). It snaps to cell corners and centres; hold Alt to place it freely. The preview disc turns around this point, and the disc size below grows to keep the whole grid covered.
