Dice Grid · dice on the table

Throw the dice into the tray. The tray is the same size as the grid and split into the same cells, and wherever the white die comes to rest picks the one cell that changes. Instead of pips it shows bands: its face is the tile that cell will get, with the same number of bands starting from the same corner, and it turns as the die turns. The grey die shows a colour sample instead of pips: whichever way up it lands, light ground with a dark band or dark ground with a light band, is how that cell is coloured. Where it lands doesn't matter. Throw with the button or Space, or press in the tray and flick.
