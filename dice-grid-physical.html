Dice Grid · physical overlay

The dice set a still grid on screen. A disc of straight black lines, printed on clear film, turns over it. The disc never changes, so the grid's bands are drawn at the disc's exact line pitch in real millimetres, and the moiré comes from the two meeting. Calibrate once below, then roll with Space, R or the button.

Preview only: a simulation of the printed disc for trying rolls without it. Turn it off when the real disc is on the screen.
