Dice Grid

Every cell gets three dice. The face die sets how many diagonal bands stack out from a corner, with a 75% chance of matching its row number, the turn die picks the corner (1 starts bottom-left), and the invert die swaps the colours on a 4–6. A striped or spiral disc spins in front of the whole grid, inverting what it covers, so its stripes and the bands interfere in a moiré pattern. Press Space or R to roll again.

Face key

  • 1 One band
  • 2 Two bands
  • 3 Three bands
  • 4 Four bands
  • 5 Five bands
  • 6 Six bands
